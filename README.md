# Dream

Everything for 3DX, in one place: https://deaeath.github.io/dream/

- **3DX World Editor**: build 3DXChat worlds in your browser. https://deaeath.github.io/3dx-world-editor/
- **The Hephaestus Forge**: every world a live channel; JARVIS builds what you ask. https://deaeath.github.io/3dx-world-editor/?forge=1
- **Hyperreal**: turn a 3DX screenshot into a real-looking photo, or paint over anything and say what to change. Free. In the SBS Discord: `/hyperreal`. https://deaeath.github.io/hyperreal/
- **3DX Avatar Editor**: dress an avatar in the game's own clothes. https://deaeath.github.io/3dx-world-editor/avatar.html
- **3DX Profile Studio**: write your profile like a document. https://deaeath.github.io/3dx-profile-studio/
- **3DX Radio Streamer**: your own internet radio station. https://github.com/Deaeath/3dx-radio-streamer
- **3DXModKit & 3dxGC**: a client-side mod framework, and the game's memory kept tidy. https://github.com/Deaeath/3DXModKit
- **The Kink List**: rate every kink and share your list (adults only). https://sbsbot.my.pebble.host/kinklist
- **3DXChat Toolkit**: coming soon.
